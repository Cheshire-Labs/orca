# In memory of Joanne Hull Salmi

My mom, Joanne Hull Salmi, passed away on November 19th, 2025, during the development of this
project. I'm thankful I had the ability, and made the decision, to move back to California from
Boston to be with her during her final months and to be by her side in her final moments.

There's no real way to sum up who my mom was or what she meant to me. Anything I write here will
fall short. What I can say is she's shaped my life in ways I'll carry forever. I strived to make
her proud, and she was a constant cheerleader in my life despite many mistakes I've made. It will
be difficult knowing I won't have her steady support as I continue forward.

It's easy to lose hours and work away your life in devotion to some company or project, as I've
done here. But the time we share with the people we love is fleeting, and it's easy to lose track
as it passes. So build the type of life you want and live it now.
